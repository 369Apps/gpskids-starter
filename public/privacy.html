Privacy

GPS Kids Speak is free public-speaking practice for ages 5 to 17. This page is the short version. The full note for people who run a copy of the app is PRIVACY.md in the source.

Kids

We do not ask a child for a name, email, phone number, school, or photo. Practice stays on the phone. A take-home link can move scores to another phone, and that link does not include a name. Recordings are not uploaded to be kept. The coach turns speech into words to give a tip, then drops them.

There is no countdown clock for kids. A soft ring fills while they talk.

Grown-ups

A parent can leave a first name, an email, and a child's age band, and only after a grown-up check (a short math question). We do not ask for the child's name. A trial-class form, if you turn it on, asks for the parent's name and phone and the child's age, not the child's name.

What we count

The app can store anonymous counts, such as "a talk started", tied to a random number, so a school can see if the app is being used. Those counts are not a name, a voice, or the words a child said.

The public leaderboard is off. Pitch recordings are not stored.

Contact is whatever address a school puts in public/brand.js. This copy does not publish a personal email or a street address.
