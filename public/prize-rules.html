GPS Kids speaking practice

Practice is free. Points, if you use them, stay on this phone.

Who it is for

  • Kids ages 5 to 17.
  • A parent or guardian starts anything that asks for contact details.

Points on this phone

  • Your first challenge each day: 10 points.
  • Did it yesterday too: +5 points.
  • Speak clearly, at a good speed, with confidence: up to +10 points.
  • Only the first challenge each day counts. After that it is practice.
