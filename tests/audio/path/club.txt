What if every kid got one hour every week to build anything they want?
Here is my case. Every kid should get a build hour, because building makes you brave.
For example, last week I built a cardboard robot. It fell apart two times, and I fixed it both times.
That taught me to keep trying, even when things break.
A build hour also helps kids work as a team, because you have to share ideas and tools.
So give every kid a build hour. I am the full package, and so are they!
