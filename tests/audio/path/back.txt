Have you ever wondered how a funny cat video gets to your phone in one second?
Here is my big idea. The internet is a giant team of computers that pass your video along in tiny pieces called packets.
First, the video lives on a big computer called a server.
Then the server chops the video into little packets, and each packet travels through cables, even under the ocean, to your house.
Finally, your phone puts the packets back together, and the video plays. So the internet is like super fast mail for video. Thank you!
