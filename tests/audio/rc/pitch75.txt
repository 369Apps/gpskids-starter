Have you ever lost a shoe right before school and made your whole family late?
That happens in my house almost every morning, and it drives my mom crazy.
My idea is the Beep Shelf. It is a shoe shelf with little sensors, and it beeps at night if one shoe is missing, so you can find it before bedtime instead of in the morning rush.
Here is why it works. Last week we were ten minutes late because my little brother could not find his left sneaker. It was under the couch the whole time. With the Beep Shelf, we would have found it the night before.
It is cheap to make, it uses a tiny battery that lasts a whole year, and it is easy to use. Even my four year old brother understands it.
I already built a test version out of a cardboard box, a buzzer, and an old toy remote, and it worked three nights in a row. My grandma even said she would buy two of them, one for her house and one for ours.
Every family with kids needs one. Mornings would be calmer, nobody would be late, and parents would stop yelling about shoes.
So please say yes to the Beep Shelf, and never be late again. Thank you!
