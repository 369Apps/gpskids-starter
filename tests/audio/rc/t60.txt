Have you ever wondered how a funny cat video gets to your phone in, like, one second?
Here is my big idea. The internet is basically a giant team of computers that pass your video along in tiny pieces called packets.
First, the video lives on a big computer called a server, maybe really far away.
When you press play, your phone sends a request through your Wi-Fi router to your internet company.
Then the server chops the video into thousands of little packets, and each packet travels through cables, even cables under the ocean, until it reaches your house.
For example, when I watch a soccer highlight, the first few seconds load first, so I can start watching while the rest is still on the way. That is called buffering.
Finally, your phone puts all the packets back together in the right order, and boom, the video plays.
So the internet is like a super fast mail system for tiny pieces of video. Thank you!
